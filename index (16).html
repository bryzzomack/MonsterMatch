    How to play
    1. Pass the device to the player whose monster is on screen.
    2. Tap Roll the dice. The dice show a number and a body part: eyes, arms or legs.
    3. Tap every monster with exactly that many. The dots show how many are hiding.
    4. Find them all and the dice roll again. Keep going until the timer runs out.

    Scoring

    Kid Ages 5 to 7. 9 monsters and 60 seconds. Every monster found is 10 points, however long it takes. Three in a row earns a star worth 10 more. Sparkly gold super monsters are worth 20 extra, but only if they match, so count first! A wrong tap costs 1.5 seconds and no points.
    Grown-up 12 monsters and 30 seconds. A find is worth 10 points if it takes 3.5 seconds or less. Slower finds are worth less, down to 3. Sometimes two pairs of dice roll: the monster must match both, and it's worth double. A wrong tap costs 5 points and 2 seconds.
    Expert 16 monsters and 30 seconds. Zero counts, more double rolls, and only 2.5 seconds for full points. A wrong tap costs 5 points and 3 seconds.

    With Catch-up time on, a player who is behind gets 1 extra second for every 8 points behind the leader, up to 20 seconds.

    Playing alone

    In Monster rain, monsters drift down from the clouds. Tap the ones that match before they float away. In Peekaboo, monsters pop up from the grass and duck back down. Catch the matching ones while they're up. Speed round switches between the grid, rain and peekaboo every roll.

    When playing alone, the dice sometimes roll a color too, like "green monsters with 3 eyes". Finishing a roll adds time (5 seconds for kids), and the best score for each mode is saved on this device. Practice has no clock at all, and kids always get the count-along. Tap I'm done to finish.

    Every gold super monster a kid finds gets a name and joins the Monster collection. Tap Adopt under one to make it a pet.

    Monster Town

    A whole town to explore: Town Square, Whisper Woods, Frosty Peak, Sunny Beach, Pumpkin Farm, the Funfair and Treasure Island. Hold a finger on the screen to walk (or tap where to go, or use the arrow keys). Monsters with a yellow ! have missions: pick up and count apples, deliver to the right house number, paint the barn the right color, race the go-kart, sail to Treasure Island and more. Answer a wild monster's question (count my eyes, what color am I, what letter does my name start with) to make a friend who can follow you around. Spend coins in the shops by counting them out one at a time. Also look for treasure chests, the wishing fountain, bus stops, the hot air balloon, nighttime fireworks, rainbows and surprises.

    Monster Studio and Coloring Book

    In the Studio, pick a monster, color its body, rub on lipstick, blush and eye shadow, dress it up, add stickers and drawings, snap photos and win the fashion show by counting the judges' stars. Looks are saved to your pets, so they wear them in Monster Town too. The Coloring Book has color-by-number pages (each number is a color, read out loud) and free coloring with crayons, markers, glitter and stickers.

    Monster safari

    Explore four places and make friends with 20 shy monsters. Tap or drag on the ground to walk. Walk too close and monsters run away, so throw the treat in a monster's thought bubble and it comes over to eat. While it's munching, tap it to blow a friendship bubble around it. Wiggly bushes and rocks have someone hiding inside: tap them to look. Every friend fills a spot in the Monster Book. Find 3 friends in a place to open the next one, and watch for rare sparkly monsters.

    My monster

    Make a monster by picking its color and counting out its eyes, arms and legs. Then take care of it: feed it the number of treats it asks for, rub off the mud in the bath, dress it up, and count stars at bedtime. Each one earns a heart, and hearts unlock new things to wear. Its needs go down slowly over the day but never run out.

    Monster trail

    Take turns rolling the die. Kids tap Hop once for every dot while the game counts along; grown-ups hop automatically. Rockets zoom you ahead 3, puddles slide you back 2, stars are for collecting, and question spaces ask you to count a monster for 2 bonus hops. First one home wins. With one player, add the computer buddy.
